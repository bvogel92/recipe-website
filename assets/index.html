Sync across your devices

Sign in with Google to keep the same recipes on your phone, tablet and computer. They are saved to a private app folder in your own Google Drive.

  • Only this app can open that folder. It can’t see your other Drive files.
  • This website has no server, so your recipes go only between your browser and your Google Drive.
  • Sign in with the same Google account on each device. Meal plans and colors stay on each device.
