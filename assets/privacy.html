Privacy

Recipe Collection runs entirely in your web browser. This website has no server or database, and the people who run it cannot see your recipes.

What is stored, and where

Your recipes, photos, ratings, meal plans and color choice are saved in your browser on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them, so use Export to keep a backup.

If you turn on sync

When you choose Sync and sign in with Google, your recipes are copied to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive so your other devices can load them. The app asks Google for two things only: access to that hidden folder, and your email address so it can show which account is syncing. It cannot open any of your other Drive files. Your recipes travel directly between your browser and Google.

To stop, choose Stop syncing here. To remove the synced copy, choose Delete synced copy, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections, where you can also delete its hidden data.

Importing recipes

When you import a recipe, the page you import from is read in your browser. If this site has an import helper set up, the recipe page's address is sent to that helper so it can fetch the page; nothing else is kept.
